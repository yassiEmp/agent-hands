# Launch 01 — your agent, your real browser, in the background

- Date: 22 Oct 2026, with release 0.19.0
- Story: an AI agent works in the browser you are signed into, without taking
  your screen, and hands sign-ins (passkeys included) back to you.
- Number: Google accepted a real sign-in in a launched Edge (HANDOFF.md, 22 Oct).
- Clip: `docs/assets/hero.gif` for now. Better: a 20 s recording of a handoff

## X thread (posted by the owner, @yassi_yari)

1/ (attach hero.gif)

I built agent-hands. It lets your AI agent work in your real browser, the one you are already signed into, in the background while you keep working.

It clicks and types like a person, so sites do not flag your accounts.

Free and open source: github.com/yassiEmp/agent-hands

2/

When the agent reaches a sign-in, the window comes up for you, after you stop typing. You sign in, and it carries on by itself.

Passkeys too: the agent presses the button, you touch the fingerprint reader.

3/

Long text is pasted the way you would paste it. In rich editors it is a real Ctrl+V, and your clipboard is given back.

Works with Claude Code:
npm i -g agent-hands
